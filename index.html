Built around AI

Other systems add AI as a chat window on the side. OpenGunstore was designed for it: every job in the store is a tool an AI assistant can run. We want this to be the new standard, with routine work automated and people making the calls.

What is MCPThe Model Context Protocol, an open standard that lets AI assistants use other software. OpenGunstore has an MCP server built in
What it can doLook up stock, receive invoices, take payments, start a 4473, list guns online, run sales and tax reports. Over 100 tools
Which assistantsClaude, ChatGPT and any other assistant that supports MCP. Each person signs in with their own POS login
What it costsNothing extra from us. Claude or ChatGPT plans start at about $20 a month per person

Features

In daily use at Old Steel Arsenal, an independent FFL dealer in Plano, Texas. Built on Frappe and ERPNext v16.

Point of Sale Storage Locations Cash Drawer Form 4473 Records
CounterSerial picking, license scanning, order queues
Form 4473Sale is blocked until the 4473 is complete and NICS says Proceed. Used guns can require a signed agreement
Bound bookElectronic bound book, synced to FastBound
PaymentsPayroc today. Any other card processor can be added: build it yourself or ask us. Saved cards, split payments, several readers, daily reconciliation
Web and auctionWooCommerce and GunBroker from one inventory
DistributorsRSR, Sports South and Lipsey's ordering and drop-ship
Cash drawerDaily count with variance check
Back roomConsignment, trade-ins, stock counts, storage map
MembershipsPlans, member prices, renewal follow-up
Pre-ordersTake full payment or a deposit, deliver when stock arrives
GunsmithingWork orders from intake to pickup
CRMFrappe CRM with team mail, want lists and held guns

Who it's for
